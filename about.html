WHO WE ARE

Committed to Your Family's Wellbeing

Al-Shifa International Hospital DHA Lahore was founded with a singular mission: to bring transparent, ethical, and international-standard medical care to our community. Our state-of-the-art facility integrates premium surgical departments, intensive care wards, and advanced diagnostics under the guidance of top clinical experts.

As a JCI-accredited facility, we enforce rigorous sterilization, clinical safety, and documentation standards modeled directly on global benchmarks, ensuring peace of mind for every patient.
