Frequently Asked Questions

You can book an appointment online using the form on this page, or call our 24/7 reception desk at +92 42 35771100. Alternatively, send us a message on WhatsApp at +92 300 1234567 for immediate confirmation.
Al-Shifa International Hospital is empanelled with all major insurance companies in Pakistan, including Jubilee Life, EFU Life Takaful, Adamjee Insurance, IGI General, Pak-Qatar Takaful, and the Sehat Sahulat Program.
OPD clinics operate from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM Monday through Saturday. Individual consultant timings vary by doctor and are listed on our Doctors page. Emergency and Pharmacy are open 24/7.
Lab and radiology reports can be collected in person from our 24/7 diagnostics counter or requested directly via WhatsApp. Enter your MR number on our patient portal for instant PDF downloads.
General ward visiting hours are 4:00 PM – 7:00 PM daily (maximum 2 attendants per patient). For ICU & CCU patients, strictly 1 attendant is allowed during designated 15-minute slots at 11:00 AM and 5:00 PM to ensure maximum infection control.
