THE AL-SHIFA STORY

Founded on Principles of Patient First Care

Al-Shifa International Hospital was established in 2008 with a simple yet ambitious vision: to build a medical institute where patients in Pakistan receive world-class tertiary healthcare without having to travel abroad. Starting as a 50-bed cardiac specialty hospital in DHA Lahore, our founders prioritized clinical integrity, foreign-trained clinical leadership, and strict compliance with international safety protocols.

Today, Al-Shifa has expanded into a flagship 250+ bed tertiary care hospital equipped with 15+ medical departments, Level-III NICU facilities, digital catheterization labs, and Pakistan's premier robotic knee replacement suites.
