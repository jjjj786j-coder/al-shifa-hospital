The Al-Shifa Story

16 Years of Unwavering Medical Excellence

Founded in 2008 at Main Boulevard, DHA Phase 5, Lahore, Al-Shifa International Hospital began as a specialized cardiac center with a vision to eliminate the need for patients to travel abroad for critical surgeries.

Over the past 16 years, Al-Shifa has expanded into a flagship 250+ bed tertiary care institute offering 15+ specialized departments, 24/7 Level-III NICU, robotic surgery, and direct insurance cashless billing.
